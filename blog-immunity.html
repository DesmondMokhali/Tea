The Invisible Shield: Keeping Flu and City Dust Out of Your Household

Mei Huang, MHSc
Holistic Health Educator
4 June 2025
5 min read

In a busy modern city, your immune system is constantly exposed to environmental stressors. When work stress and physical exhaustion deplete your reserves, your Wei Qi (Defensive Qi) drops. In traditional medicine, Wei Qi acts as a protective shield that circulates just beneath the skin, keeping external pathogens like wind, cold, and city dust from invading your system.

When your Wei Qi is weak, cold and flu strains can easily take root. To protect your household, you must strengthen this defensive shield before seasonal bugs strike. This is a practical checklist to ensure your home's biological defenses are fully prepared.

Pre-Season Biological Readiness Checklist

  • Nourish Wei Qi daily to build a strong defensive shield.
  • Clear toxic heat from the lungs to soothe dry throat and nose.
  • Keep botanical remedies on hand to address early cold symptoms.
