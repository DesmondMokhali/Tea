The 28-Day Hormone Blueprint: Achieving Flow State, Not Monthly Burnout

Mei Huang, MHSc
Holistic Health Educator
4 June 2025
6 min read

Do you ever feel like you're fighting your own body for one week every month?

Your patience disappears. Small problems feel overwhelming. Cravings intensify. Energy drops. Sleep becomes unpredictable. Then, just as you're starting to feel like yourself again, the cycle repeats.

Many women assume this is simply something they have to endure. Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) takes a different view. Rather than treating monthly fluctuations as an inconvenience, it views the menstrual cycle as valuable feedback about the body's overall balance.

In TCM theory, emotional tension, PMS discomfort, bloating, and irritability are often associated with a pattern known as Liver Qi Stagnation. Meanwhile, fatigue, brain fog, and feeling depleted after menstruation are commonly linked to insufficient Qi and Blood replenishment.

The goal isn't to suppress your cycle. The goal is to support your body through each phase so you can maintain steadier energy, greater resilience, and a stronger sense of wellbeing throughout the month.

Common Questions About Monthly Energy and Hormone Balance

Executive Client
"A week before my cycle, I become bloated, irritable, and emotionally drained. Why does it feel like my stress levels suddenly double?"
Holistic Health Educator
"Many women experience this pattern. In Traditional Chinese Medicine, these symptoms are often associated with Liver Qi Stagnation—a state where the body's natural flow becomes restricted. This may contribute to feelings of tension, irritability, discomfort, and emotional overwhelm."
Executive Client
"Why do I feel completely drained after my cycle ends, even when I've been sleeping well?"
Holistic Health Educator
"Traditional medicine views the post-cycle phase as a time of rebuilding. During this period, the body benefits from support that helps replenish Qi and Blood. When recovery is insufficient, many women report fatigue, reduced focus, low motivation, and difficulty maintaining energy throughout the day."
Executive Client
"So the goal isn't to fight my cycle?"
Holistic Health Educator
"Exactly. The goal is to work with your body's natural rhythms. When support is aligned with the cycle, many women experience better comfort, steadier energy, and a greater sense of balance throughout the month."
